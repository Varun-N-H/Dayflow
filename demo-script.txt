# Dayflow HRMS — 5-Minute Video Demo Script
================================================================================
Everything you need for your hackathon video walkthrough: 
Exact On-Screen Actions + Word-for-Word Voiceover Script.
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[0:00 – 0:45] PHASE 1: INTRODUCTION & THE CORE PROBLEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCREEN ACTION:
- Start on the Sign In page (http://localhost:3000/signin).
- Move the cursor over the clean card and the "Don't have an Account? Sign Up" link.

VOICEOVER:
"Hello everyone! Welcome to Dayflow — a modern, unified Human Resource Management 
System built to align every workday perfectly.

In growing companies, managing HR operations across spreadsheets and fragmented tools 
is chaotic. Employee onboarding is slow, payroll computations are error-prone, and 
attendance and leave approvals lack visibility.

Dayflow solves this by delivering a cohesive, role-based platform that unifies 
organization registration, automated employee provisioning, live attendance tracking, 
interactive 12-month leave management, and automated Indian salary structure 
computation into one lightning-fast experience."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Click the logo upload box and upload any logo (notice the instant live image preview).
- Fill in:
    * Name: "Varun Admin"
    * Email: "admin@odooindia.com"
    * Password: "password123"
    * Confirm Password: "password123"

VOICEOVER:
"Let's start by onboarding a new organization. As an HR Administrator or Founder, 
I register our company, 'Odoo India', and upload our brand logo.

Under the hood, Dayflow's onboarding engine automatically computes a deterministic 
company prefix — in this case, 'OI' — sets up organizational departments, and 
provisions the initial Administrator account in a single atomic transaction."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1:30 – 2:30] PHASE 3: EMPLOYEE DIRECTORY & SALARY BREAKDOWN ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCREEN ACTION:
- You land on /employees (point out the dynamic Odoo India logo and name in the top navbar).
- Click "+ Add Employee".
- Fill in:
    * First Name: "John"
    * Last Name: "Doe"
    * Email: "john.doe@example.com"
    * Department: "Engineering"
    * Designation: "Senior Software Engineer"
    * Monthly Wage: "80000"
- Click "Create Employee".
- Show the success modal displaying the deterministic Login ID ("OIJODO20260001") 
  and temporary password.
- Click on John Doe's card to open his full profile (/profile).
- Click the "Salary Info" tab and show the automatic statutory breakdown:
    * Basic Salary (50%): Rs. 40,000
    * HRA (50% of Basic): Rs. 20,000
    * Standard Allowance: Rs. 4,167
    * Provident Fund (12%): Rs. 4,800
    * Professional Tax: Rs. 200

VOICEOVER:
"Now we are on the Admin Dashboard. Notice how the navbar immediately displays 
our company logo and active session.

Let's add our first employee. When we enter John Doe's details and set a monthly 
gross wage of 80,000 rupees, Dayflow's deterministic ID generator provisions John 
with a unique login ID — 'OIJODO20260001' — and a secure temporary password.

If we inspect John's profile under the Salary Info tab, Dayflow's automated statutory 
engine has already calculated the complete Indian CTC breakdown — 50% Basic Salary, 
50% HRA, Provident Fund at 12%, Professional Tax, and Performance Bonuses with 
zero manual calculation."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[2:30 – 3:30] PHASE 4: LIVE SYSTRAY ATTENDANCE & DAILY LEDGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCREEN ACTION:
- Move cursor to the top right of the navbar to the Systray Widget.
- Click "Check IN ->" — watch the badge turn to pulsing emerald "Checked In".
- Click the "Attendance" tab in the navbar.
- Show the Daily Ledger table displaying all company employees with their live 
  status badges (Present, Absent, On Leave) and check-in timestamp.
- Click "Check OUT ->" in the top right to demonstrate instantaneous status update.

VOICEOVER:
"Next, let's explore Attendance Tracking. Dayflow features a persistent, one-click 
Systray widget at the top right.

With one tap on 'Check IN', our timestamp is recorded and our live status turns 
to an active emerald indicator. Navigating to the Attendance Ledger, HR Admins have 
a real-time overview of the entire workforce for any selected date — tracking live 
presence, total working hours, and overtime calculations seamlessly."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[3:30 – 4:15] PHASE 5: 12-MONTH INTERACTIVE TIME-OFF & APPROVAL QUEUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCREEN ACTION:
- Click the "Time Off" tab in the navbar.
- Point out the 12-Month Interactive Calendar Grid and remaining leave balances 
  (24 Paid Time Off, 7 Sick Days).
- Click on any upcoming date on the calendar or click "Request Time Off".
- Select Leave Type: "Paid Time Off", pick start/end dates, add remark: "Family Vacation", 
  and submit.
- Show the request appearing in the Pending Requests Queue below.
- Click the green "Approve" checkmark button to demonstrate instant approval.

VOICEOVER:
"For leave management, Dayflow provides a comprehensive 12-Month Annual Calendar view. 
Employees and HR can visualize holidays and booked time off across the entire year at a glance.

When an employee requests time off, leave quotas are validated in real time against their 
annual allocation. As an Admin, the request appears directly in our centralized Approval Queue, 
where we can approve or reject leaves with a single click."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[4:15 – 4:45] PHASE 6: ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL & EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCREEN ACTION:
- Click the user avatar at top right -> Click "Sign Out".
- On /signin, enter John's credentials:
    * Login ID / Email: "OIJODO20260001"
    * Password: (enter the temporary password generated earlier)
- Show the forced Security / Password Reset screen on first login.
- Point out that John's view is Employee Self-Service:
    * The "+ Add Employee" button is gone.
    * The Salary Info tab is locked and hidden.
    * John sees only his personal monthly attendance and leave requests.

VOICEOVER:
"Now let's verify our Role-Based Access Control. When John Doe logs in using his 
system-generated ID and temporary password, Dayflow automatically enforces a 
first-time security password reset.

Once logged in as an Employee, the UI adapts: administrative controls are locked down, 
confidential salary structures are restricted, and the user receives a clean, self-service 
dashboard dedicated to their personal schedule and attendance."


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

VOICEOVER:

Thank you for watching the Dayflow demo!"
